What to expect

Turnaround

  • Original tracks (~3–4 min): about one week
  • Covers: 2–3 working days
  • Specific deadlines agreed during project discussion

Deliverables

  • .wav renders of final mixes
  • Reference masters & premasters
  • Acapella and full instrumentals
  • Extra renders (stems, stage edits) may incur additional fees
  • Project files are not typically provided

Licensing & rights

  • You retain commercial and personal use rights to your commissioned work unless otherwise negotiated
  • Rights to original compositions are discussed case by case where Gower has contributed significantly to the composition

Good to know

  • Standalone vocal tuning/timing is included within full productions, not sold separately
  • Standalone external mastering isn't offered; reference masters are delivered to a professional standard
  • Complex projects, tight deadlines, poor-quality source audio, or copyright considerations may affect pricing
