Project · July 2022

MeloFade v1

A DIY Arduino MIDI CC controller build.

I had been thinking for a while that the MIDI CC controllers I've been using have had either faders that were too short, or drivers that were unstable and prone to crashing. MeloFade v1 was my attempt at designing a custom solution — longer, smoother faders and rock-solid drivers.
